FAQ

Quick Answers

What is KongMerlin?

A collection of 1,111 meme ape wizards on BNB Chain — part mighty kong, part legendary Merlin. Every token is a unique portrait with its own expression, hat, staff, robe and background: smug grins, laser eyes, diamond hands and more.

How much does it cost to mint?

Two lanes draw from the same 1,111 supply. The first 500 free mints are fully claimed, so the mint() lane now costs 0.0001 BNB per mint. Paid Mint: 0.002 BNB per mint, available from the moment mint opens, for as long as supply remains. Fees go to the contract. Plus normal BNB Chain gas.

Can I pick which KongMerlin I get?

No — minting is a random draw. You never choose a number; the draw hands you one of the 1,111 wizards, and yours is revealed once the mint transaction confirms.

Do I need a Binance Agent ID to mint?

Yes — for minting only. Your wallet must hold a Binance Agent ID, Binance's soulbound identity: one per eligible wallet, and it can never be transferred or lent. After you connect, this page checks your wallet's balance in the Agent ID registry (0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F) on BNB Chain — no Agent ID, no mint. Brewing in the Potion Lab and buying from the Potion Shop need no Agent ID; every wallet is welcome there.

How do I mint?

Connect a wallet that holds a Binance Agent ID on this page and mint directly on either lane, or copy one of the agent prompts above — one per lane — and let your AI agent mint for you from an Agent ID wallet.

When does mint open?

Mint is open. The contract is deployed on BNB Chain at 0xad14617AbcB15058E226A107D19eaDcA08bBA51B, and this page reads the live mint status from the contract — the MINT STATUS line above and the mint buttons always follow the on-chain state. Brewing and the Potion Shop are open too.
